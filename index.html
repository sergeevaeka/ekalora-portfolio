About Ekaterina

Where Natural Beauty, Elegance & Innovation Meet

I am Ekaterina — a commercial model, tech entrepreneur, and experienced event organizer, combining my passions for fashion, natural beauty, contemporary art, a healthy lifestyle, and innovation.

My modeling style is defined by natural elegance, graceful femininity, and refined aesthetics. With an elegant neckline, beautifully defined collarbones, graceful hands, glowing skin, and an athletic physique, I bring a sophisticated presence to luxury commercials, jewelry and watch campaigns, beauty and skincare advertising, and premium brand events. I embrace 100% natural beauty and believe in a healthy, active lifestyle, fitness, and skincare.

Beyond modeling, I hold a Master’s Degree in Engineering and have over 15 years of experience in engineering, IT, and technology leadership, spanning the automotive, pharmaceutical, industrial automation, and payment technology industries. Today, I am the founder of The Miracle Tree, an AI technology company.

I also bring extensive experience in public speaking, conference organization, brand presentations, and corporate events. Having led teams responsible for technical communications, digital presence, UI/UX, product quality, and professional events, I have also organized beauty and technology conferences, coordinated branded exhibition stands, and managed brand experiences.

I am especially interested in collaborations involving fine jewelry, luxury watches, elegant fashion, premium skincare and beauty, wellness and skincare, fitness and activewear, automotive brands, and innovative technology and lifestyle products, as well as luxury events, exhibitions, product presentations, and corporate gatherings.

Combining a model’s visual presence, an engineer’s analytical mindset, and an event professional’s communication skills, I bring elegance, intelligence, creativity, and professionalism to every collaboration.
